ASA: Where lost meets found.

Web-based Lost & Found Tracking System for CSU Main Campus

ASA (Cebuano for "Where") replaces manual bulletin boards and scattered online posts — offering one trusted platform to report, claim, and reunite lost belongings with their owners.

About ASA

Developed to solve inefficiencies in the current lost-and-found process at CSU Main Campus, ASA digitizes everything from lost item reporting to ownership verification. Whether you're a student, staff, or visitor, the system provides real-time item listings, claim workflows, and administrative oversight to ensure each item is tracked responsibly.

In Version 1.0, ASA implements a centralized digital solution that reduces unrecovered items, improves transparency, and empowers campus security to manage claims with structured guidelines.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide an efficient, user-centric lost and found platform that bridges the gap between loss and recovery, fostering a responsible and honest campus environment.

Vision: A campus where every lost item has a digital footprint, every claimant is verified fairly, and no item remains unrecovered due to lack of communication.

"Efficiency. Transparency. Accountability. The core behind ASA."

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: React, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript – responsive, mobile-friendly UI.
  • Backend: Node.js + Express.js – REST API architecture.
  • Database: MySQL 9.x – stores users, items, claims, and logs.
  • Deployment: Ubuntu 24.04 LTS cloud server.
  • Security: bcrypt password hashing, HTTPS, parameterized queries.

The system is designed to scale within existing free hosting constraints while maintaining fast performance under normal campus usage.
