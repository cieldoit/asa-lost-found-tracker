Privacy Policy & Community Safety

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use ASA:

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Details of lost or found items you post
  • Messages between users

How We Use Your Information

  • Help users find lost items or return found items
  • Communicate important updates
  • Improve our website and services
  • Keep the platform safe and secure

Prohibited Content

The following are not allowed on the platform:

  • Spam or unrelated posts
  • Fake or misleading information
  • Offensive, abusive, or harmful content
  • Selling products or advertisements

Enforcement

To keep ASA safe, we apply the following rules:

Warning: For first violations.

Suspension: For repeated violations.

Permanent Ban: For serious or repeated abuse.
